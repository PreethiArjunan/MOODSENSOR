Reset & Heal 🌿
Self-care 🛁

Healing isn’t a race — it’s a quiet, gentle journey. Some days it looks like tears and rest. Some days it feels like peace and a warm cup of tea. ☕💚

"Little by little, your soul remembers how to bloom again."

Your heart has carried so much. You deserve softness, patience, and time to breathe. Little by little, you grow again.

Unplug from noise. Stretch your body. Drink water. Sit under the sky. Call someone who feels like home. Healing begins in small moments.

Forgive yourself for survival mode. You did your best with what you had. That itself is strength.

Life blooms again when you do. Trust your timing. You are becoming whole again. 🌱
